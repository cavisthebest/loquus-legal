Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

The short version: Loquus has no servers and no user accounts. Everything you do in the app stays on your device. We don't collect your data because there's no system on our end for it to go to.

What Loquus is

Loquus is a language-learning app for iOS. It works entirely offline: lessons, your progress, your streak, and everything else Loquus knows about your learning are stored only on your device. Loquus does not have a server, a database, or any backend that your information could be sent to.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account. Loquus does not ask for your name, email address, or any sign-in. There is nothing to create and nothing we could tie your usage to.
  • No analytics or tracking. Loquus does not use any analytics, advertising, or tracking software, and does not share data with third parties for advertising purposes, because it does not collect data in the first place.
  • No ads. Loquus has no advertising and no ad networks.

What's stored, and where

Your learning progress (which words you know, your streak, your preferences, and similar) is saved in a file on your own device, the same way any other app saves its local settings. It is never transmitted anywhere. If you delete Loquus from your device, that data is deleted with it. Loquus does not back this data up to any server of ours.

Microphone and speech recognition

Loquus optionally uses your device's microphone so you can practise saying words aloud and get feedback on your pronunciation. This uses Apple's on-device speech recognition. Your voice is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere, including to us. You can decline microphone access and still use every other part of the app.

Purchases and subscriptions

Loquus offers optional subscriptions ("Loquus Plus") through Apple's App Store. All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple. Loquus never sees or stores your payment details, card number, or billing address. What we receive from Apple is limited to whether your subscription is currently active, which is used only to unlock the relevant features in the app.

Children's privacy

Loquus is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because we do not collect information from any user regardless of age.

Your rights

Because nothing is collected or stored outside your device, there is no external record of your data for us to give you, correct, or delete on request. Deleting the app deletes everything Loquus has stored about your learning. If you have questions about this, or believe this policy doesn't fully answer something you're required to be told under your local law (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date. Because Loquus doesn't collect contact information from users, we have no way to notify you directly, so please check back if you have concerns.
