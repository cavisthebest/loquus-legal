Common questions

How do I get more than 3 lessons a day?

Loquus Plus removes the daily lesson limit, and also unlocks section reviews, the Arcade, and deep progress tracking. You can start a free trial or subscribe from the upgrade screen in the app.

How do I manage or cancel my Loquus Plus subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed entirely by Apple. On your device, open Settings, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, and select Loquus. Cancelling stops future renewals; it doesn't refund the current period. Loquus itself has no way to see or change your billing, since all payment handling happens on Apple's side.

Which languages does Loquus support?

Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin Chinese, and Dutch, including a separate Belgian Dutch track.

Is my data private? Where is it stored?

Everything Loquus knows about your learning stays on your device. There's no account and no server. See the Privacy Policy for the full details.
